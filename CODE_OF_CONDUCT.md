# Community conduct

Discussion around OSImageDeploy should be professional, constructive, and
focused on improving understanding of the product.

Participants must not harass others, publish personal or confidential
information, encourage unsafe destructive testing, submit malicious content,
or use project channels for advertising or unrelated promotion. Technical
disagreement is welcome when it is expressed respectfully and supported by
useful evidence.

x9000.com Consulting Services Limited may edit, hide, lock, or remove content
and may restrict participation where necessary to protect people, systems, the
project, or its channels.

Private security concerns must be reported through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md),
not through a public issue.
